FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers for store owners and managers.

How does Ekwandeni handle SAB and craft brewery crate deposit changes? keyboard_arrow_down
Deposit prices can be updated centrally in head office or locally by an authorized manager. When SAB increases crate deposit rates, you schedule the date and time; all tills adjust simultaneously. Furthermore, Ekwandeni tracks previous vs current deposit rates so older return slips are cashed out at the correct historical value.
What happens to card payments and till transactions if load shedding strikes mid-shift? keyboard_arrow_down
Card speedpoints connect via fallback dual-SIM LTE or direct USB/Bluetooth tether to the till terminal. If mobile towers go down entirely, cash and verified account billing continue uninterrupted. Receipts print via battery-backed thermal printers, and the entire queue of transactions is saved encrypted to the local SQLite database.
Can we manage multiple stores with different pricing tiers across provinces? keyboard_arrow_down
Yes. The multi-store cloud controller allows you to segment branches by pricing zone (e.g. Gauteng Central vs Western Cape Coast vs KZN Rural). Promotional bundles, local craft lines, and price tiers can be assigned per branch or grouped regionally while retaining centralized inventory oversight.
How long does migration from legacy systems like storeOS or GAAP take? keyboard_arrow_down
We offer white-glove onboarding. Our South African technical team extracts your historical SKUs, barcodes, customer debtor ledgers, and supplier catalogs in 48 hours. Most stores run in parallel for a single shift and go fully live over a single weekend with zero downtime.
