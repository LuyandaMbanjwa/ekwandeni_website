# Ekwandeni POS — Stitch design system

Reference: [umkhoai.com](https://www.umkhoai.com) typography and editorial rhythm; Ekwandeni brand cyan from storeOS.

## Brand

Purpose-built POS for South African alcohol retailers: deposits, empties, offline till, multi-store back office. Editorial serif headlines with warm sans body; red accent words sparingly (like UmkhoAI).

## Typography

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line height |
|------|--------|--------|------|-------------|
| Display / H1 | Libre Baskerville | 700 | clamp 2.5–4.25rem | 1.08 |
| Section H2 | Libre Baskerville | 700 | clamp 1.875–2.75rem | 1.12 |
| Lead | Almarai | 400 | 1.25rem | 1.65 |
| Body | Almarai | 400 | 1rem | 1.65 |

## Color roles

- Canvas dark: `#0c0a09`
- Canvas light: `#fafaf9`
- Brand primary: `#1f9cc8`
- Editorial accent: `#e80c2a`

## Layout

- Max width 1400px; asymmetric hero and one-of-a-kind split (copy | 3D panel)
- No overlapping text on imagery; no scroll-hint UI

## Motion intent

Fade-up entrances; 3D panel is decorative with reduced-motion fallback.
