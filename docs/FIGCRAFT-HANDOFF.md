- **Mobile:** 390 × 844

1. **Display** — Libre Baskerville Bold 68 / 72 auto, tracking -1%
2. **H2** — Libre Baskerville Bold 44
3. **Lead** — Almarai Regular 20, fill `#57534e`
4. **Body** — Almarai Regular 16
5. **Nav** — Almarai Bold 14

- FAQ accordion: single border container, radius 12
